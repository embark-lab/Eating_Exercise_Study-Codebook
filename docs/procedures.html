Exercise & Eating Study - Study Procedures and Measures

Chapter 1 Procedures

1.1 Recruitment and Compensation

The Exercise & Eating study recruits from both an undergraduate and community sample recruited via the University of Texas at Austin SONA system and flyers distributed to clinical settings and on social media in the United States. Following determination of eligibility via a pre-screen form administered to all students in the SONA system, participants were invited to complete a battery of self-report questionnaires on exercise and eating motivations and behaviors, body image, mood, and demographics. The surveys were estimated to take approximately 1-2 hours to complete in total. Otherwise, participants in the community were able to access the study survey directly, which was a shorter version estimated to take approximately 30 minutes. Participants were compensated either via research credits toward an introductory psychology course (if enrolled through SONA) or by entering a raffle for a $20 gift card.
