BDI-II bdi (24 variables)

The Beck Depression Inventory-II (Beck, Steer, & Brown, 1996) is a 21-item self-report questionnaire measuring severity of depressive symptoms over the past two weeks. Each item is a group of four statements scored 0–3.

Scoring

A total sum score (0–63) is computed, with higher scores indicating more severe depressive symptoms. No REDCap calculated field is included for the BDI-II in SONA; score from the item variables.

ED 100K PLUS ed_100k_plus (308 variables · 78 scored)

The ED100k-Plus is a lab-adapted version of the ED100K.V3 (Thornton et al., 2018), a self-report eating disorder assessment based on the SCID-5. It assesses current and lifetime eating disorder symptoms and includes items from the Dieting and Weight History Questionnaire (variables beginning wdqhq_), lab ED-history items (edhistory_, dcf_), EDE-Q items (edeq_), and EDDS items (edds_). It covers weight history (current, highest, lowest, set-point weight and BMI), menstrual history, binge eating, compensatory behaviors, driven exercise, restriction, and weight/shape concern. This measure is not yet validated.

Scoring

Weights entered in kilograms are converted to pounds (*_correct_plus), BMIs and weight-suppression values are computed, and diagnostic variables follow DSM-5 algorithms for threshold and sub-clinical (past and current) Anorexia Nervosa, Atypical Anorexia Nervosa, Bulimia Nervosa, Binge Eating Disorder, and Purging Disorder. Diagnostic variables are coded 0 = criteria not met, 1 = criteria met.
