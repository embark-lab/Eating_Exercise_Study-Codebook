Study Overview

The Exercise & Eating Study is an online survey study of exercise and eating motivations and behaviors.

The survey covers demographics, medical and treatment history, alcohol and drug use, height and weight; exercise habits and motivations; thoughts and feelings related to exercise, body image, weight gain, self-esteem, mood, mental health, and self-worth; how exercise may affect relationships with others; and hypothetical scenarios asking whether participants would keep exercising under different conditions (e.g., when sick or injured). No directly identifiable information (e.g., name, date of birth) is collected.
