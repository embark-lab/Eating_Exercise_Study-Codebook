The **Eating Disorder Fear Questionnaire** (EFQ) is a 20-item self-report measure of fears related to eating, weight gain, and their consequences, rated 0 (*Not at all*) to 6 (*Very much so*). It yields a total score and five subscales: Fear of Weight Gain, Fear of Social Consequences, Fear of Personal Consequences, Fear of Physical Sensations, and Fear of Social Eating.

**Key Variables** (calculated in REDCap)

- `efq_sum` — EFQ Sum Score
- `efq_fowg` — average of EFQ Fear of Weight Gain questions
- `efq_fosc` — average of EFQ Fear of Social Consequences questions
- `efq_fopc` — average of EFQ Fear of Personal Consequences questions
- `efq_fops` — average of EFQ Fear of Physical Sensations questions
- `efq_fose` — average of EFQ Fear of Social Eating questions

**Attention checks:** `efq_ac` — exclude from scoring.
