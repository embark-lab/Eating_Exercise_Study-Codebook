The **Drive for Muscularity Scale** (DMS; McCreary & Sasse, 2000) is a 15-item questionnaire assessing attitudes and behaviors reflecting a desire to be more muscular. In SONA, items are coded 5 (*Always*) to 0 (*Never*), so higher scores indicate greater drive for muscularity.

Administered in the *Exercise Questions Compiled* form; all variables carry the suffix `_merged`.

**Key Variables** (calculated in REDCap)

- `dfm_average_merged` — Drive for muscularity Average

**Attention checks:** `dfm_ac_merged` — exclude from scoring.
