The **Eating Habits Questionnaire** (EHQ; Gleaves, Graham, & Ambwani, 2013) assesses orthorexic (healthy-eating–focused) attitudes and behaviors, including knowledge of healthy eating, problems associated with healthy eating, and feeling positively about healthy eating. Items are rated 1 (*False, not at all true*) to 4 (*Very true*).

**Attention checks:** `ehq_attention` — exclude from scoring.
