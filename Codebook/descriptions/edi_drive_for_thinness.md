The **Eating Disorder Inventory – Drive for Thinness** subscale (EDI-DT; Garner, Olmstead, & Polivy, 1983) is a 7-item measure of excessive concern with dieting, preoccupation with weight, and fear of weight gain. In SONA, items are coded 0 (*Never or rarely*) to 4 (*Always*); `edi_sweets` is reverse-keyed in REDCap.

> ⚠️ **Check before analysis:** the REDCap calculation for `edi_total` includes the attention-check item `edi_attention` in the sum. Recompute the total from the seven substantive items.

**Key Variables** (calculated in REDCap)

- `edi_total` — EDI Total

**Attention checks:** `edi_attention` — exclude from scoring.
