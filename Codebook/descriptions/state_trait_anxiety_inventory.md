The **State-Trait Anxiety Inventory** (STAI; Spielberger et al., 1983) is a 40-item self-report measure. Items 1–20 assess state anxiety (how one feels *right now*; *Not at all* to *Very much so*) and items 21–40 assess trait anxiety (how one *generally* feels; *Almost never* to *Almost always*). In SONA, items are coded 0–3.

**Key Variables** (calculated in REDCap)

- `stai_sum` — STAI sum score.
- `stai_clinical_cutoff` — Clinical Anxiety cut-off met?

**Attention checks:** `stai_ac` — exclude from scoring.
