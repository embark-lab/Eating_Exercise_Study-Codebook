The **Exercise Dependence Scale** (EDS-21; Hausenblas & Symons Downs, 2002) is a 21-item measure of exercise dependence symptoms based on DSM substance-dependence criteria, rated from *Never* to *Always* (coded 0–5 in SONA). It has seven subscales: Withdrawal, Continuance, Tolerance, Lack of Control, Reduction in Other Activities, Time, and Intention Effects.

Administered in the *Exercise Questions Compiled* form; all variables carry the suffix `_merged`.

**Key Variables** (calculated in REDCap)

- `eds_mean_merged` — Exercise Dependence Scale Mean Score
- `eds_withdrawal_effects_merged` — Withdrawal effects score
- `eds_continuance_merged` — Continuance score
- `eds_tolerance_merged` — Tolerance score
- `eds_lack_control_merged` — Lack of control score
- `eds_reduction_activities_merged` — Reduction of other activities score
- `eds_time_merged` — Time score
- `eds_intention_merged` — Intention effects score

**Attention checks:** `eds_ac_merged` — exclude from scoring.
