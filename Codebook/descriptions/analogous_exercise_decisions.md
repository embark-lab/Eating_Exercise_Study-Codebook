The **Analogous Exercise Decisions** (AED – Brief) task is a lab-developed behavioral-economic questionnaire assessing preferences between exercise and alternative rewards. Participants choose between a planned workout (30 minutes or 1 hour) and increasing amounts of money, and indicate whether they would eat increasing additional snack amounts (kcal) in order to exercise for 30 minutes or 1 hour.

**Scoring**

Breakpoints are determined for each series as the first point at which the participant chooses the alternative over exercise (money series) or declines the snack requirement (snack series). No REDCap calculated fields are included.

**Attention checks:** `aed_ac1` — exclude from scoring.
