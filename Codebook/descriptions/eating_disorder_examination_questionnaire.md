The **Eating Disorder Examination Questionnaire 6.0** (EDE-Q; Fairburn & Beglin, 2008) is a 28-item self-report version of the EDE assessing eating disorder attitudes and behaviors over the past 28 days. Items 1–12 and 19–21 use frequency/proportion ratings (0–6), items 13–18 are behavior counts, and items 22–28 are severity ratings (0 = *Not at all* to 6 = *Markedly*).

**Scoring**

Four subscales — Restraint, Eating Concern, Shape Concern, Weight Concern — are computed as item means, and the global score is the mean of the four subscales. No REDCap calculated fields are included.

**Attention checks:** `edeq_attention` — exclude from scoring.
