The **Functional Assessment of Driven Exercise** (FADE) assesses the functions that exercise serves, adapted from the four-function model of behavior (automatic/social × positive/negative reinforcement). Participants rate how often they exercise for each reason from 0 (*Never*) to 3 (*Often*).

Note: `fade_17_attention` (*To get attention*) and `fade_24_avoid_attention` are substantive items, not attention checks.

**Key Variables** (calculated in REDCap)

- `fade_apr_score` — Automatic Positive Reinforcement Score
- `fade_anr_score` — Automatic Negative Reinforcement Score
- `fade_spr_score` — Social Positive Reinforcement Score
- `fade_snr_score` — Social Negative Reinforcement Score

**Attention checks:** `fade_ac` — exclude from scoring.
