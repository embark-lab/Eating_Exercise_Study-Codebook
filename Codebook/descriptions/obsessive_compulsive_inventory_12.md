The **Obsessive-Compulsive Inventory-12** (OCI-12; Abramovitch, Abramowitz, & McKay, 2021) is a 12-item self-report measure of obsessive-compulsive symptoms (checking, ordering, obsessing, washing) rated 0 (*Not at all*) to 4 (*Extremely*) for distress over the past month.

**Key Variables** (calculated in REDCap)

- `oci_sum_score` — OCI Sum score
- `oci_clinical_cutoff` — Clinical cut-off Met?

**Attention checks:** `oci_ac` — exclude from scoring.
