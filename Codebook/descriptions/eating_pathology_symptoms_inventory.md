The **Eating Pathology Symptoms Inventory** (EPSI; Forbush et al., 2013) is a 45-item self-report measure of eating disorder symptoms over the past four weeks, rated 0 (*Never*) to 4 (*Very often*). It yields eight subscales: Body Dissatisfaction, Binge Eating, Cognitive Restraint, Purging, Restricting, Excessive Exercise, Negative Attitudes toward Obesity, and Muscle Building.

**Note on `_merged` variables:** the Excessive Exercise and Muscle Building items were also administered within the *Exercise Questions Compiled* form; those copies carry the suffix `_merged` (e.g. `epsi_5_exercise_alot_merged`) and are listed here alongside the main EPSI form.

**Key Variables** (calculated in REDCap)

- `epsi_body_dissatisfaction` — Body Dissatisfaction Sum Score
- `epsi_binge` — Binge Sum Score
- `epsi_restraint` — Cognitive Restraint sum score
- `epsi_purging` — Purging sum score
- `epsi_restrict` — Restricting sum score
- `epsi_exercise` — Excessive Exercise sum score
- `epsi_neg_obese` — Negative attitudes toward obesity sum score
- `epsi_muscle_build` — Muscle building sum score

**Attention checks:** `epsi_ac` — exclude from scoring.
