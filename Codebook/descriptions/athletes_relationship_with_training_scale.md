The **Athletes' Relationship with Training Scale** (ART) is a 15-item self-report questionnaire assessing unhealthy training behaviors and beliefs. Items are rated 0–4 (frequency or agreement), with a −9 *N/A* option for items that do not apply.

**Key Variables** (calculated in REDCap)

- `art_sum_score` — ART Sum score
- `art_affect_driven_score` — Affect-Driven Training Score
- `art_training_score` — Training Amount Score
- `art_against_medical_score` — Training Against Medical Advice score
- `art_body_dis_score` — Body Dissatisfaction score

**Attention checks:** `art_ac` — exclude from scoring.
