The **Godin Leisure-Time Exercise Questionnaire** (Godin & Shephard, 1985) asks how many times in a typical week the participant does strenuous, moderate, and mild exercise for more than 15 minutes during free time.

**Scoring**

A weekly leisure activity score is conventionally computed as (9 × strenuous) + (5 × moderate) + (3 × mild). No REDCap calculated field is included.
