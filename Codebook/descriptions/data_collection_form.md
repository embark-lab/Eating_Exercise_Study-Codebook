The brief **Data Collection Form** (DCF) captures demographics and history: age, sex assigned at birth, gender identity, transgender identity, pronouns, sexual identity, race, Hispanic ethnicity, education, medical and psychiatric history and treatment, ADHD/autism diagnoses, medical conditions, marijuana, alcohol, and steroid use (including quit attempts), and family history of eating and other psychiatric disorders.

Fields `dcf_mj_age_1`…`dcf_relate_dx_5` are cells of embedded tables and have no label of their own in REDCap; their labels here were derived from the table they belong to (see the *Label Source* column in `sona_codebook.xlsx`).

**Attention checks:** `dcf_attention` — exclude from scoring.
