Lab-developed **hypothetical scenarios** assessing persistence of exercise under barriers. Sixteen scenarios are grouped into four domains — Sickness/Feeling Unwell (`sick`), Injury (`injury`), Life Interference (`interfere`), and Social (`social`), four scenarios each. For each scenario `hypo_<n>_<domain>`:

- `_1` — would you still find a way to exercise? (yes/no)
- `_intent` — if yes, intention for exercising (checkbox: mental health, routine, physical fitness, weight control, shape control, other), with `_intent_other` free text
- `_2` — if the scenario prevented exercise, how much would it bother you (0–10)

**Attention checks:** `hypo_attention` — exclude from scoring.
